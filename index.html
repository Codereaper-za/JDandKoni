The Company

JD AND KONI is an independent, black-owned company founded to serve the specialized needs of general supply of goods and services. Our objective is to provide practical distribution products and services that deliver competitive advantage to our customers, while improving companies and organizations and delivering significant cost-saving products.

We pride ourselves on understanding the unique construction and logistics industry processes in the world we operate in. The knowledge, commitment, and depth of experience of JD AND KONI’s employees enable us to tailor effective solutions designed to add maximum value to your business.

Our Philosophy

  • Compassionate and respectful relations with fellow workers, customers, and suppliers.
  • Strict adherence to the laws and ethics governing the conduct of business.
  • Supplying and producing high-quality, innovative products and services that improve our customers’ businesses.
  • Supporting our families and maintaining a healthy balance between work and life.
  • Creating a work environment that nurtures learning and personal growth.
  • Achieving business success measured by customer and employee satisfaction, while ensuring financial returns to our owners.
